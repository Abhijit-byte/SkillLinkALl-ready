About

Acclaimed Sitar player and composer bridging the gap between Hindustani classical traditions and contemporary global music. With over 15 years of performance experience across international stages, I aim to explore the expressive potential of the sitar through original compositions, fusion projects, and film scoring. Trained under renowned gurus, I am dedicated to preserving the rich heritage of Indian classical music while pushing its creative boundaries. Also passionate about teaching and mentoring aspiring musicians.
